# Glossary

ACS
: American Community Survey of the Census Bureau; PUMS is its Public Use Microdata Sample.

ATUS
: American Time Use Survey.

BIC
: Bayesian information criterion, used to choose between specifications.

CVAE
: Conditional variational autoencoder: a neural network that learns a distribution of households conditional on
  PUMA and household size and generates new households from it.

Episode
: One activity of a person on the simulated day, with a type, start time, duration and location.

GEH
: A statistic comparing hourly modelled and counted flows; values below 5 are a common target.

Hold-out half
: The half of the travel-survey households not used in any estimation or calibration step.

Inclusive value (logsum)
: The expected maximum utility of a lower-level choice, passed up to a higher-level choice.

IPF, IPU
: Iterative proportional fitting and updating: reweighting methods of population synthesis.

LODES
: Longitudinal Employer–Household Dynamics Origin–Destination Employment Statistics.

MATSim
: Multi-Agent Transport Simulation, the assignment software.

MSTM
: Maryland Statewide Transportation Model; source of the zone system and the car skims.

PUMA
: Public Use Microdata Area, the smallest geography of the PUMS.

%RMSE
: Root-mean-square error of modelled against counted volumes, as a percentage of the mean count.

RTS
: The 2017/18 Regional Travel Survey of the Baltimore and Washington regions.

SILO
: Simple Integrated Land Use Orchestrator, the land-use engine.

Skim
: A zone-to-zone table of travel time, distance or cost.

SRMSE
: Standardised root-mean-square error between two contingency tables.

TASHA
: Travel Activity Scheduler for Household Agents, whose scheduling approach ORIOLE-Day adopts.

TVD
: Total variation distance between two distributions, in percentage points.

WESML
: Weighted exogenous sample maximum likelihood, the estimator for choice-based samples.
