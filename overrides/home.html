Maryland Integrated Land-use and Everyday-travel Simulator

From census records
to cars on the road

MILES is an agent-based modelling platform for Maryland. It synthesises every household of the region, moves it through the housing and job markets year by year, plans each person's weekday and simulates the resulting traffic on the road and transit networks.

The platform

Five models, one chain

Each stage hands its output to the next. Land use runs year by year from 2016 to 2024; activities and traffic are simulated for a weekday of 2024. Select a stage.

Synthetic population

A conditional variational autoencoder generates households and persons of 2016, including combinations of attributes that the sample does not contain, then places them in zones and dwellings and gives workers a job.

Engine
Conditional VAE, Python and PyTorch
Trained on
ACS PUMS 2012–16, 253,029 households in 96 PUMAs
Produces
Households, persons, dwellings, jobs and workplaces of 2016

Model designValidation

Tested on

Held-out 10 % of ACS PUMS records; ACS 2014–18 by PUMA

Land use

SILO moves the population through time: births, deaths, marriages, residential moves, job changes and car ownership, with MILES's estimated residential-location, workplace and car-ownership models.

Engine
SILO, Java, yearly steps 2016 → 2024
Sensitive to
Car times and accessibility, housing costs, vacancies, jobs, incomes
Produces
Population, dwellings and jobs of every year

Model designEstimatesValidation

Tested on

Held-out ACS PUMS movers; ACS and Census 2021–24, years not used in calibration

School location

Every student is assigned a school in each SILO year with three multinomial logits, respecting attendance boundaries, enrolment and capacity.

Engine
Inside SILO, every simulated year
Models
K–8, high school, college

Activities: MAST

Each person receives a full weekday: activities drawn from survey distributions, scheduled, located with estimated logit models, and tour modes chosen with household car allocation and joint tours.

Produces
One weekday plan per person: activities, locations, times, modes

OpenStreetMap roads 2024; GTFS transit

Estimated and tested, not tuned

27 choice models estimated with Biogeme; every stage tested on data it never saw. Counts play no role in estimation or calibration.

Validation

Tested on data it never saw

The travel survey is split into an estimation half and a hold-out half; land use is calibrated on 2017–20 and tested on 2021–24; traffic is compared with 2024 counts that enter no step of the model.

Command line

Install and run

MILES installs as a Python package with one command, miles, for every step of the chain. Java engines and data paths are set once in miles.toml.

Installation Running the chain

# get the code
$ git clone https://github.com/Tomal121186621/MILES.git
$ cd MILES && pip install -e .

# point MILES to the data and engines
$ cp miles.example.toml miles.toml
$ miles config

# run the chain, stage by stage
$ miles population all
$ miles landuse run
$ miles activity run
$ miles assignment run
