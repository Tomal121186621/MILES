    County view¶

    The validation of every stage, county by county: all 24 Maryland counties for the population and land use, and the six counties of the study area (Baltimore City and the counties of Baltimore, Anne Arundel, Howard, Harford and Carroll) for every stage.

    How to read the figures

    Heatmaps: total variation distance between the model and the ACS for each county and variable, in percentage points; bold above the 5 pp limit. The six study counties are above the line. Bars: orange model, dark grey observed. County samples of the ACS are small, so differences of a few points in the smaller counties are within its sampling error.

    Synthetic population, 2016¶

    The base population against the ACS PUMS 2014–2018, by county. 151 of 168 county × variable cells are within 5 pp, 16 between 5 and 10, one above 10 (household size in Baltimore City, where one-person households are too few).

    Land use, 2024¶

    The 2024 population of the land-use model (mean of five seeds) against the ACS PUMS 2024, by county, after eight years of simulation. 131 of 168 cells are within 5 pp, 35 between 5 and 10, two above 10.
