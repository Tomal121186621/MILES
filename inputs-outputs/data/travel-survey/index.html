    Regional Travel Survey 2017/18¶

    The 2017/18 Regional Travel Survey (RTS) is the household travel survey of the Baltimore and Washington regions. MILES uses it to estimate the activity models (tour formation, scheduling, destination, stop and mode choice), the workplace and school location models, and to validate the activity model on households not used in estimation. This page gives the codebook of the variables MILES reads and aggregate statistics of the survey.

    Source and access¶

    Item Value
    Producer Baltimore Metropolitan Council (BMC) and the National Capital Region Transportation Planning Board (TPB) at the Metropolitan Washington Council of Governments [15]
    Survey period Weekday travel days, October 2017 to October 2018
    Design Household survey, one assigned weekday travel day per household; all members report their trips
    Files read One flat file in which every trip row carries its household and person fields; a cleaned trip file; a tour file built from the cleaned trips; the codebook (sheets Household, Person, Vehicle, Trip)
    Weights wthhfin (household), wtperfin (person), wttrdfin (trip)
    Survey zone systems BMC TAZ 2010 and TPB TAZ 3722 [16]
    Access Released by the agencies on request. The survey files are not redistributed with MILES.

    The tables show aggregate statistics only. Shares are weighted with the weight of the unit of observation; a share or statistic resting on fewer than 10 unweighted records is shown as "—".

    Statistics¶

    Sample¶

    The flat file has 488,705 rows: the household, person, trip and vehicle tables are joined, so trip records repeat. Removing the repeats gives 163,290 trips of 41,914 persons in 21,788 households, the same records as the cleaned trip file. The files hold only persons with at least one trip on the travel day (trips_yesno = 1 for every record). Weighted, 13.6 % of household members made no trip; their records are not in the files. The tour file holds 57,627 tours, of which 54,149 are closed home-based tours or work-based subtours and 2,342 are open.

    Survey zone systems¶

    The survey codes locations by 2010 census tract and by the zones of the two regional models. The BMC TAZ 2010 layer has 2,922 zones (1,387 in the BMC region, the rest copies of TPB zones); the TPB TAZ 3722 layer has 3,669 zones. MILES places survey locations in its own zones through the census tract (internal point of the 2010 tract), because neither survey zone system has a crosswalk to the statewide model zones.

    Processing¶

    • Trips: the cleaned trip file (deduplicated); purpose from o_activity and d_activity as above; mode from travel_mode: auto driver and motorcycle to car driver; auto passenger, school bus, paratransit, taxi and ride-hailing to car passenger; rail to rail; bus and private bus to bus; air, water and other dropped from mode shares.
    • Person types: worker (employment_status 0, a working student counts as a worker); student (student_status 1), by age band 5–10, 11–13, 14–17, 18+; retiree (employment_status 1); non-worker otherwise; persons under 5 have no tours.
    • Tours: closed home-based tours and work-based subtours of the tour file; open tours are dropped.
    • Split: households are split into an estimation half and a validation half, stratified by home county and household size (1 to 5 or more), with a fixed seed. Models are estimated on one half and validated on the other.
    • Survey population: home zone = the statewide model zone containing the internal point of the household's 2010 census tract; income = midpoint of hh_income_detailed; household members without a trip are added from hhsize, numworkers and numstudents, with age, gender and licence copied from a traveller of the same role.
    • Workplace and school: the zone of the work or school tract when it lies inside the zone system.
    • Parking: the survey records whether a driver paid for parking but not the amount; the car-driver utility uses the share of paid parking at the destination area.
