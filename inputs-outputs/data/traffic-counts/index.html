    Traffic counts¶

    Traffic counts are used only to test the assignment; no parameter is fitted to them. Three sources are used: the MDOT SHA count stations (annual average daily traffic), the FHWA continuous count stations (hourly volumes) and the MDOT SHA vehicle-miles of travel by county and functional class.

    MDOT SHA annual average daily traffic¶

    Source. MDOT State Highway Administration, Annual Average Daily Traffic, statewide count stations 2015–2024 [23], downloaded from the MDOT SHA open data portal. The file has 8,189 stations in Maryland; 3,797 lie in the six study-area counties.

    Variable Description Type Values / categories / units Used in

    COUNTY_DESC, ROADNAME, ID_PREFIX, ID_RTE_NO, STATION_DESC County, road, route and location string — Road identity in matching
    F_SYSTEM Functional system integer 1 Interstate … 7 local Matching tolerance, strata, split
    MAIN_LINE Mainline or ramp integer 1 mainline; other values ramps Ramp stations matched to ramp links
    NUM_LANES Lanes at the station, both directions integer lanes Lane check

    AAWDT_2024 Annual average weekday traffic, 2024 integer vehicles per weekday Weekday conversion
    COUNTED_FACTORED Counted in the year or factored from an earlier count string C counted; F factored Reported
    CAR_AADT, LIGHT_TRUCK_AADT, BUS_AADT, SINGLE_UNIT_AADT, COMBINATION_UNIT_AADT AADT by vehicle class integer vehicles per day; classified stations only Passenger-vehicle share

    Processing¶

    • Each direction of a station is matched to the nearest car link of a compatible class within 60 m (functional system 1–2) or 40 m (others), with the road identity checked against the MDOT route and the OSM name; ramp stations match ramp links only. A two-way count with one direction found takes the opposite carriageway within 150 m.
    • The matched stations with a 2024 AADT are split once, with a fixed seed and stratified by functional system, into a calibration-check half and a held-out half; the held-out half is compared only once, in the final validation.
    • Model volumes are compared with passenger vehicles: AADT × (1 − truck share − bus share) of the station, or of its class where the station has no classification.

    FHWA continuous count stations (TMAS)¶

    Source. Federal Highway Administration, Travel Monitoring Analysis System, 2024 monthly volume files and station file for Maryland [25]. Volume records give hourly counts by station, direction, lane and day.

    Codebook¶

    Variable Description Type Values / categories / units Used in
    station_id Continuous station string 0Pnnnn = MDOT station Pnnnn Join with the MDOT station and its links
    county_code County FIPS (station file) string 003, 005, 013, 025, 027, 510 in the study area Station selection
    f_system Functional system and area type string 1–7 with U urban / R rural Road groups
    travel_dir Direction integer 1 north, 3 east, 5 south, 7 west Link of the direction
    travel_lane Lane integer 1 = outside lane Summed over lanes
    month_record, day_record, day_of_week Date integer day_of_week 1 Sunday … 7 Saturday Weekday selection
    hour_00 … hour_23 Hourly volume integer vehicles per hour; negative = missing Hourly profile, test

    Processing¶

    • Weekdays Monday–Friday, federal holidays of 2024 and the day after Thanksgiving excluded; days with a missing hour or a missing lane are dropped, and days below half the median daily total of the station-direction are dropped.
    • Average weekday = mean over the kept days of the volume summed over lanes, per hour and direction. No year scaling (the counts and the model are both 2024, with the Key Bridge closed from 26 March).

    MDOT SHA vehicle-miles of travel¶

    Source. MDOT SHA, 2024 Mileage Reports (as of 1 January 2025), table FCAVMT-ALL, annual vehicle-miles of travel by county and functional class on all public roads [26]. The PDF is converted to text and parsed.

    Codebook¶

    Variable Description Type Values / categories / units Used in
    County row County of Maryland string 23 counties and Baltimore City County totals
    Columns 1–7, rural and urban Annual VMT by functional class float millions of vehicle-miles per year Summed over rural and urban
    TOTAL, GRAND TOTAL Totals float millions of vehicle-miles per year Check

    Processing¶

    • Annual VMT ÷ 365 gives the average day; the weekday factor (AAWDT ÷ AADT) and the passenger-vehicle share of each class come from the count stations of the six counties (collectors and local roads pooled).
