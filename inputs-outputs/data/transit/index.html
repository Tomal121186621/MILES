    Transit¶

    The transit network of the assignment and the transit skims of the land-use and activity models are built from the GTFS timetables of the operators in the network area. Fares enter the mode-choice utilities; boardings from the National Transit Database are a validation target only.

    GTFS timetables of 18 feeds¶

    Source. MTA Maryland developer resources (five MTA feeds) and the official feeds of 13 regional operators, with the Mobility Database as mirror where the official URL failed [24]. Downloaded 21–22 October 2026. One representative weekday, Wednesday 21 October 2026, is extracted from every feed.

    Variable Description Type Values / categories / units Used in
    routes.txt: route_id, agency_id, route_short_name, route_type Route and its mode string / integer route_type 0 tram (light rail), 1 subway, 2 rail, 3 bus, 4 ferry Lines of the MATSim schedule, vehicle types
    trips.txt: trip_id, route_id, service_id, direction_id, shape_id One scheduled vehicle trip string — Departures
    stop_times.txt: trip_id, stop_id, stop_sequence, arrival_time, departure_time Timetable of each trip string / time hhss, may exceed 24:00 Route profiles, schedule
    stops.txt: stop_id, stop_lat, stop_lon, parent_station Stop location float WGS84 degrees Stop facilities, clip to the network area
    calendar.txt, calendar_dates.txt: service_id, weekday flags, start_date, end_date, exception_type Days of service date / integer YYYYMMDD; 1 added, 2 removed Selection of the weekday
    frequencies.txt: trip_id, start_time, end_time, headway_secs Frequency-based service time / integer seconds Expanded to departures
    shapes.txt: shape_id, shape_pt_lat, shape_pt_lon, shape_pt_sequence Route geometry float WGS84 degrees Mapping routes on the road network
    transfers.txt: from_stop_id, to_stop_id, min_transfer_time Transfer rules string / integer seconds Transit router

    Processing¶

    • Active services of the day: calendar range with the Wednesday flag, plus exceptions added, minus exceptions removed. Five feeds whose calendar had ended are used on their last Wednesday with service (marked in the table).
    • Stops farther than 250 m from a node of the road or rail network are dropped (the network covers Maryland, not DC or Virginia); trips keep their stops inside the area, and trips with fewer than two such stops are dropped. Amtrak Thruway buses are dropped.
    • All identifiers are prefixed with the feed key and the feeds are merged; pt2matsim converts the merged feed and maps routes on the road network guided by the GTFS shapes.

    Transit fares¶

    Source. Published fare tables of MTA Maryland and WMATA for 2017/18 [20]. The fares are coded as rules in the mode-choice model (one-way fare per half-tour, 2017 dollars, the year of the travel survey); the car operating cost of the same model is $0.179 per mile (AAA, Your Driving Costs 2017 [19]).

    Service Fare rule (2017 dollars, one way) Applied to
    MTA local bus, Light RailLink, Metro SubwayLink $1.80 single-trip fare (from 25 June 2017) All transit paths not covered below
    WMATA Metrorail, peak $2.25 up to 3 miles, rising linearly to $6.00 at 15 miles or more of car distance Rail paths with both ends in the WMATA counties
    WMATA Metrorail, off-peak $2.00 to $3.85, same interpolation Same, outside the AM and PM periods
    Metrobus and county buses $2.00 Bus paths with both ends in the WMATA counties
    MARC train min($8.00, $4.00 + km ÷ 15) Rail paths longer than 30 km

    WMATA counties: District of Columbia, Montgomery and Prince George's (Maryland), Arlington, Fairfax, Loudoun and Prince William (Virginia), and the independent cities of Alexandria, Fairfax, Falls Church, Manassas and Manassas Park. The car distance of the zone pair is taken from the car-distance skim. The drive-access leg of park-and-ride paths adds its car operating cost.

    National Transit Database, MTA Maryland¶

    Source. Federal Transit Administration, National Transit Database [27]: Complete Monthly Ridership (2002–2026) and Annual Data – Service by Mode and Time Period (report years 2022–2025), MTA Maryland (NTD ID 30034), downloaded from data.transportation.gov.

    Processing¶

    • Weekday target: Average Typical Weekday boardings of the mode, all types of service, report year 2024.
    • 2017/18 equivalent: the 2024 weekday value × (mean annual boardings 2017–2018 ÷ annual boardings 2024) of the mode.
    • Demand response is not modelled and not compared.
