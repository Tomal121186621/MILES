    Data¶

    MILES is built from public data, with two exceptions marked † (released by the agency on request). The tables list the data by the stage that uses them; bracketed numbers refer to the sources at the end of the page. The role of a dataset is estimation (a model is estimated on it), input (used as given), target (a calibrated parameter is matched to it) or test (used only for validation).

    Files are placed in the workspace under data/raw/<source>/. The instruction sheet of each model in the estimation package lists the exact files and variables.

    Dataset Source Years Role

    Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey Bureau of Labor Statistics [4] 2016 Input: vacant jobs

    GTFS timetables of 18 operators MTA Maryland and regional operators [24] 2026 Input: transit network and skims
